# Privacy

Always Active Plus does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data,
browsing activity, interaction data, telemetry, or technical data.

The extension stores its enabled state, hostname inclusion or exception list,
event-blocking preferences, and hostname policies locally in Firefox through
the extension storage API. This information remains on the user's device and
is used only to configure the extension.

Opening Project Home, Report Bug, Test Rules, or another external link from the
options page is an explicit user action and follows the destination site's own
privacy policy.
